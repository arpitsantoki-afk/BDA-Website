Architecture does not end at the drawing board. We follow every project through to its physical conclusion — by design and by choice.

01.

Architecture + Landscape

Considered, context-aware design across every typology — residential, commercial, hospitality, and landscape. We treat each site as a unique proposition, shaping built form and outdoor environments in concert. Never a formula, always a place.

02.

Interior

Interior design conceived as an extension of the architecture, not an afterthought. Material, light, proportion, and atmosphere — resolved from structure to surface.

An Extension of Our Practice

OBLIQUE Collection

Custom solid wood furniture, born from the projects we love.

While designing and executing spaces for our clients, we became so absorbed in the character of solid wood that we began crafting bespoke loose furniture to complete them. Unikraft is that obsession, formalised.

Every piece is custom-designed, solid wood, and made in India. Blue Door clients have direct access to Unikraft — the furniture conceived alongside the space it lives in.

How a project unfolds

A clear, disciplined process that keeps the design intent alive from the first meeting to the final walkthrough.

01

Discover

We begin by listening. Site, brief, budget, aspiration — understood in full before a single line is drawn.

02

Design

Concept sketches evolve into detailed drawings and spatial studies — refined iteratively until the design earns its place.

03

Approve

Regulatory submissions, planning approvals, and permits — navigated entirely by our team.

04

Build & Hand Over

Construction, fit-out, and quality sign-off managed by the same studio. The space you receive is the space we designed.
